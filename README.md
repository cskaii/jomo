# JOMO — Joy of Missing Out

**Less scrolling. More living.**

JOMO helps you build a healthier relationship with technology by making room for offline activities, meaningful connections and everyday moments. Turn completed activities into a growing personal garden, or work together with friends to nurture a shared space.

## Features

- **Your own account:** Create an account, sign in securely, recover your password and personalise your display name, time zone and daily screen-time intention. Password strength feedback helps you choose a stronger password.
- **Screen-time Insights:** Manually record daily screen time and social use, view your history and trends, and edit or remove entries. Screen time stays separate from completed offline activity minutes.
- **Offline activities:** Plan personal or group activities, join friends and use a voluntary focus timer. Review completed activity time and progress.
- **Optional activity evidence:** Choose to share a one-time approximate location during an activity or submit a private photo after it ends. Group activities support evidence review by another eligible participant. Photos and locations do not automatically prove completion.
- **Rewards and personal gardens:** Earn eligible activity coins, purchase plants, pets and decorations, and place or rearrange your collection in your garden. Moving existing items is free.
- **Shared gardens:** Grow a garden with your group. Active members use the group's collective coins and collection, with a history of earnings and purchases.
- **Friends and private groups:** Send and accept friend requests, invite people into groups and manage membership. Group owners can manage roles, remove members and transfer ownership.
- **In-app notifications:** Receive website notices for friend requests, group invitations, evidence reviews and rewards, with unread indicators and actions to respond.
- **Privacy Guardian:** Choose text, images or supported data to check for possible personal information. Review redacted results and download redacted images or data. Automated screening can miss information and does not guarantee safe sharing.
- **Privacy challenges:** Practise spotting oversharing, location clues and impersonation through short, fictional quizzes, with a cooldown between automatic prompts.
- **Personalised experience:** A first-use introduction, account-based greetings and illustrated loading screens help guide you through JOMO.
- **Control over your data:** Manage optional sharing preferences, remove saved check-ins and photo evidence, clear screening history, download account data or delete your account. A privacy notice explains how information is handled and how to contact the operator.

JOMO's website relies on the information and activities you choose to record. It does not automatically monitor other apps, block device usage or continuously track your location. Notifications appear inside the website while it is open.
